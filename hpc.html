Quantum computing

Quantum methods, on the frontier

Kronos explores quantum computing (Qiskit / VQE) for materials and encoded-operator problems — resource-estimated today on classical simulators, QPU-bound as the hardware matures. We report it honestly: quantum is a frontier we study, not a claim we lean on.
